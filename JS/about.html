OUR STORY:

  • FieldEye was founded in 2026 by Ezinhle Ndlovu with the goal to give farmers and landowners a birds-eye view of what's really happening on their land and construction projects From sprawling farmlands to busy construction sites, we saw the need for reliable mapping technology that combines high-resolution aerial photography and footage with a drone-based mapping, helping clients see their land, their property and their operations from a perspective that ground-level simply can't touch. By combining advanced drone technology with local expertise, we help our clients make smarter data driven decisions.

    • OUR VISION & MISSION:

  • Our mission is to empower farmers and property developers across South Africa with accessible, high precision aerial imaging. Our vision is to grow our business into a trusted, leading aerial intelligence comapny by bridging the gap between traditional farming and modern precision agriculture and construction
